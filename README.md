### Game Details & Guide
What is Hextris?


Hextris is a popular browser-based puzzle game where players take on the role of a hexagonal tile mover, tasked with rearranging tiles to create a grid. The goal is to score points by creating valid hexagonal grids and completing levels within a certain time limit.


How to Play


To play Hextris, follow these steps:
• Start by clicking on the starting point (center of the board) to begin.
• Use your mouse or touchscreen to move tiles around by clicking and dragging them onto adjacent empty squares.
• Each tile can only be moved once per turn. If you click on a tile that is not empty, it will remain stationary.
• To create valid hexagonal grids, ensure that each row of the grid has exactly three pieces.
• If you complete a level, your score will increase accordingly.


Tips and Tricks for Success


Here are some tips to help you improve your gameplay:
• Start with easy levels: Begin with simple levels to get a feel for the game mechanics and gain experience.
• Pay attention to grid patterns: Keep an eye on potential grid patterns that can lead to higher scores.
• Use empty tiles strategically: Use empty tiles to create temporary barriers or block other players from completing levels.
• Experiment with different tile movements: Try moving tiles in different ways to see how it affects the game board.
• Keep track of your progress: Monitor your score and level completion progress to stay motivated.
